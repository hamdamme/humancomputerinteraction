## Method notes

Session outcome counts and timing summaries are descriptive statistics. Interpret them with the task wording, participant recruitment, study setting, and research question. Do not treat a small convenience sample as representative of a wider population.

## Attribution and updates

When adding a source, record its author or issuing organization, title, publication date when available, stable URL, and license or access terms. Keep quotations brief and identify them clearly. Link to the original work instead of copying chapters or figures.
